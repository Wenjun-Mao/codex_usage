offsets, labeled median/typical range, grouped counts and a clear small-sample
note. Point, overview and end-label tooltips appear through immediate scoped CSS
hover/focus instead of browser-delayed SVG titles. Pointer hover takes priority
over retained focus; leaving it restores keyboard inspection. No product scripts,
timers, new tab stops, geometry or CSP changes. Exact buckets, raw model IDs and
source counts remain available in detailed inspection.

Account overlay tooltips lead with allowance or credit remaining, then local
capture time, readable account/source labels and the right-axis cue. These dense
account marks retain native popup timing; only their wording and local time
changed. Do not describe every dashboard tooltip as immediate.
